## 💡 My Approach

I believe educational technology should:

1. Make difficult concepts easier to understand.
2. Encourage experimentation and independent thinking.
3. Support teachers rather than replace them.
4. Make quality learning resources more accessible.
5. Connect classroom teaching with real-world applications.
